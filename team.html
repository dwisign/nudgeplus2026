The people behind
the science.

Nudgeplus is the pioneer of nudge services in Indonesia — built to close the gap between behavioural science and practice. Our team applies RCT (research, consultancy, and training) to help public and private institutions, and non-profit organisations, change their stakeholders' behaviour. We work closely with IBEF (Indonesia Behavioural Economics Forum), a network of Indonesian scholars trained at the world's top universities.

Meet Our Team

Behavioural scientists.
Economists. Specialists.

Every member of the team is trained in a different discipline — economics, psychology, engineering, public health, data science — united by the same method: KALIBER.

Lury Sofyan
Behavioural Economist

Having a PhD in Behavioural Economics from The University of Nottingham, UK, he is more than passionate about architecting choices that lead people to their best outcomes. With a dozen years of experience in government and public sector, his ideas to improve the way people decide are a gold standard.

Ridwan A. B. Prasetyo
HF/E Specialist

Pursuing a PhD in Engineering Psychology at The University of Nottingham, UK, his expertise is Human Factors (HF/E) within transport, particularly aviation — currently developing a robust way to measure pilot mental workload through brain activity as an objective indicator.
