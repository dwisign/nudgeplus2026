Track A · Self Development
Master decision-making for your personal and professional life

Four practical 1-day modules. You will leave each session with a real tool — a decision protocol, a personal schedule, a redesigned presentation, a working savings nudge — not just new knowledge.

A1Bias Buster — Why Smart People Make Bad Decisions1 Day▾

Identify the biases most likely affecting your decisions right now. Learn a 3-step pre-decision check and practise it live on a real current decision. Redesign one choice context so better choices happen automatically.

Deliverable: Personal bias profile + 3-step decision check card — ready to use the next morning
Book A1
A2Deep Work — You're Busy. But Are You Productive?1 Day▾

Design a personal deep work routine using implementation intentions, environment design, and commitment devices. Protect your most important work without damaging relationships or appearing unavailable.

Deliverable: Your personal deep work schedule — drafted and committed to live, ready to run the following week
Book A2
A3Visual Impact — Make People Remember What You Show Them1 Day▾

Apply perceptual psychology and cognitive load theory to any slide in under 2 minutes. Bring an existing presentation — leave with it redesigned live using pre-attentive attributes, contrast, and the right chart type for the right argument.

Deliverable: Your own redesigned presentation — improved during the workshop, ready to present immediately
Book A3
A4Money Mind — Why You're Bad with Money (And How to Fix It)1 Day▾

Redesign your financial environment using automatic savings transfers, commitment accounts, and salary partitioning — set up live for GoPay, OVO, BRI, and BCA. Stop irrational spending by changing the context, not your willpower.

Deliverable: Personal financial bias profile + your first automated savings nudge, activated live during the session

Track B · Company Growth · Most Popular
Apply behavioral science to marketing, hiring, meetings, and team decisions

Four practical modules for corporate and BUMN teams. Indonesian examples throughout — Tokopedia, Shopee, Gojek, Bank Mandiri. In-house delivery available for teams of 30+.

B1Smart Decisions — Stop Your Team's Predictable Mistakes1 Day▾

Name and recognise anchoring, confirmation bias, sunk cost, overconfidence, and groupthink using real Indonesian case studies. Apply a team de-biasing protocol — pre-mortem, devil's advocate, independent pre-voting — practised live on a real decision your group is currently facing.

Deliverable: Team decision audit map + de-biasing protocol card — applied live to a real organisational decision
Book B1
B2Mindfluence — Decode Customers to Sell More Effectively1 Day▾

Apply consumer biases — social proof, scarcity, anchoring, decoy effect, loss aversion — ethically to your next campaign. Live analysis of real Indonesian campaigns. Design a complete A/B experiment brief ready to hand to your developer or campaign manager.

Deliverable: Digital touchpoint audit (3 improvements) + A/B experiment brief — ready to execute
Book B2
B3Meeting Hack — Run Meetings That Actually Change Things1 Day▾

The average Indonesian professional spends 23 hours a week in meetings. Most produce no meaningful decision. Facilitate pre-mortem, silent brainstorm, and structured debate live — then redesign your most frequent recurring meetings using the 3-decision-type framework.

Deliverable: Redesigned meeting agenda template for your most common recurring meeting — tested live and immediately usable
Book B3
B4Hire Smart — Stop Hiring the Person You Like1 Day▾

Most hiring decisions in Indonesia are made within the first 90 seconds. Design a structured interview protocol for a real currently-open role — with standardised questions, behavioural anchors, and independent scoring rubrics. Run a noise audit on your team's past decisions.

Deliverable: Complete structured interview guide for a real open role + noise audit results — deployable immediately

Track C · Public Policy
Design policies and campaigns that actually change what citizens do

Four modules for government, NGO, and university audiences. Based directly on Nudgeplus projects with British Embassy Jakarta, East Java Government, Plan International, and SADAR GOWES. Bahasa Indonesia available.

C1Policy Insight — Why Aren't Citizens Complying?1 Day▾

Explain why information campaigns, fines, and regulations so often fail — using a framework your leadership will immediately recognise as true. Apply EAST to redesign any policy touchpoint. Write a one-page behavioural problem diagnosis for a real current programme during the session.

Deliverable: One-page behavioural diagnosis of a real current policy challenge — ready for your Direktur or donor report
Book C1
C2Nudge Design — Design Interventions That Change What Citizens Do1 Day▾

Use COM-B to diagnose the right intervention type. Generate intervention options using MINDSPACE and EAST together. Write a complete intervention design brief — target behaviour, mechanism, delivery channel, success metric, and ethical risk assessment.

Deliverable: Complete intervention brief — ready for internal review or donor submission
Book C2
C3Policy Lab — Does Your Policy Actually Work? Test It with an RCT1 Day▾

Design a valid RCT for a real current programme — from a Kemenkes vaccination push to a Dinas KLHK waste separation programme. Choose between a full RCT and lower-cost quasi-experimental alternatives matched to your Pemda or Kementerian constraints.

Deliverable: Complete RCT design brief — ready for internal ethics review or donor proposal
Book C3
C4Campaign Shift — Change Behaviour, Not Just Awareness1 Day▾

Conduct a 10-point behavioural audit of a real current campaign from your institution. Redesign one message using social norm messaging or loss framing. Design a lightweight WhatsApp or social media testing protocol appropriate for government budgets.

Deliverable: Behaviourally redesigned campaign message + testing protocol brief — ready for internal sign-off next week

FAQ

Common questions.

Not answered here? WhatsApp us — we respond the same day.

Do participants need prior knowledge of behavioral science?+
No. Every module is designed for participants with zero prior knowledge. The only prerequisite is willingness to think differently about why people do what they do. No behavioural science background required.
How long is each session, and can modules be combined?+
Every module is 1 full day (approximately 8 hours including breaks). Full track bundles (4 modules) are delivered across 4 separate days. You can also mix modules across tracks — contact us to discuss.
Is the training in English or Bahasa Indonesia?+
Both options are available. Public cohorts are typically in Bahasa Indonesia with English materials available. Corporate in-house sessions can be fully English, fully Bahasa, or bilingual — confirmed during booking. All 12 modules use Indonesian examples regardless of language.
What is the maximum group size?+
Public cohorts are capped at 25 participants to protect facilitation quality and the session deliverable components. Corporate in-house sessions can accommodate larger groups with adjusted facilitation — contact us for groups over 30.
What exactly is the session deliverable?+
Every module ends with a working document produced during the session — not a summary of what was discussed. Examples: A1 produces a personal bias profile and decision check card. B2 produces a digital touchpoint audit and A/B experiment brief. C1 produces a one-page behavioural policy diagnosis. You leave with a document you use the following week.
How is this different from a general behavioral economics course?+
Three differences. First, every session ends with a real deliverable you use immediately — not slides or a certificate alone. Second, all content is calibrated to the Indonesian context: Indonesian case studies, Indonesian social norms, Indonesian field data. Third, your facilitators are the same PhD researchers who ran the field experiments — the COVID-19 handwashing study (IRSA-BKF Best Paper 2022), the Plan International PARENTS programme, the national Indonesia Personality Map. You learn from people who have actually done this, not from people who teach about it.
We have a specific behaviour problem in our organisation. Should we book training or consultancy?+
Good question — and one we get often. Training builds capability so your team can diagnose and solve behaviour problems independently. Consultancy is when you want Nudgeplus to run the KALIBER cycle on your behalf and hand you a verified, scale-ready solution. If you have a specific behaviour change challenge with a defined timeline and need a result — start with Consultancy. If you want your team to be able to do this themselves in future — start with Training. Many clients do Consultancy first, then send their team through Training to maintain the capability. We'll give you an honest recommendation on the free matching call.
I don't know which track or module is right. What should I do?+
WhatsApp us with two sentences about your role and what you're trying to improve. We'll match you to the right track in a free 15-minute call — and if training isn't the right solution, we'll tell you that too.
