Our Proprietary Method
KALIBER

Hacking behaviour for a better life. KALIBER is Nudgeplus's proprietary 7-step Behavioural Change Method — developed over 6+ years of field work across Indonesia, grounded in 60+ years of peer-reviewed behavioral science.

Every engagement — research, consultancy, or training — is structured around KALIBER. No other behavioral science firm in Indonesia operates this framework.

Full KALIBER explanation → © Nudgeplus · All rights reserved
K(K)ey Behaviour — Define what to changeStep 1▾
Identify the specific, measurable behaviour to target. Align all stakeholders before fieldwork begins. Most behaviour change projects fail not because change is impossible — but because everyone is trying to change a different behaviour.
A(A)udit — Observe, map, understandStep 2▾
360° field assessment: direct observation, FGD, interview, and survey. User segmentation, empathy mapping per persona, and a step-by-step Key Behaviour Map — showing what people actually do, not what they say they do.
L(L)everaging & (L)owering — Find the leverSteps 3–4▾
Identify what enables the behaviour (Leveraging Factors) and what blocks it (Lowering Factors). The priority factor becomes the intervention target — preventing the most common mistake: designing around the wrong barrier.
I(I)nsight Tools — Design the nudgeStep 5▾
Select the behaviour change mechanism using MINDSPACE and EAST — Messenger, Incentive, Norms, Defaults, Salience, Priming, Affect, Commitment, or Ego. Design the intervention and state the testable hypothesis it rests on.
B(B)ehavioural Experiment — Test before scaleStep 6▾
Run a controlled pilot — field RCT, A/B test, or quasi-experimental design. We never recommend scaling an untested intervention. Statistical power is calculated. Ethics protocol applied where required.
R(R)eflection — Scale what works. Iterate what doesn't.Step 7▾
If the experiment confirms the hypothesis: scale-up playbook ready for your team. If not: return to K with a revised hypothesis. KALIBER is a cycle — most breakthroughs come on the second or third pass.
