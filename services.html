Our Proprietary Method
KALIBER

Hacking behaviour for a better life. KALIBER is Nudgeplus's proprietary 7-step Behavioural Change Method — developed over 6+ years of field work across Indonesia. Every engagement we deliver is structured around KALIBER. It is not a theory. It is an operational protocol grounded in 60+ years of peer-reviewed behavioral science, calibrated for the Indonesian context.

Research and consultancy are not two separate services here. They are two phases of the same KALIBER cycle — we run the diagnosis before we design the intervention, and we test the intervention before we recommend scaling it.

© Proprietary framework — all rights reserved.
KALIBER was developed by Nudgeplus and is the exclusive intellectual property of Nudgeplus. No other behavioral science firm in Indonesia operates this framework.
KKey Behaviour — Define what we're changingAlways first▾
Identify 3–5 candidate behaviours and align all stakeholders on a single specific, measurable target. Most projects fail here — not because change is impossible, but because every stakeholder is trying to change a different behaviour. We resolve that before any fieldwork begins.
ABehavioural Audit — Observe, map, understandCore diagnostic▾
360° field assessment: direct observation, FGD, interview, survey, and literature meta-analysis. We segment the population, map user personas using empathy frameworks (Says/Thinks/Feels/Does), and produce a step-by-step Key Behaviour Map — showing what people actually do, not what they say they do.
LLeveraging & Lowering — Find the leverDiagnosis▾
Identify and rank the factors that help people perform the behaviour (Leveraging Factors) and those that block it (Lowering Factors). The priority factor — the one with the highest impact and tractability — becomes the intervention target. This prevents the most common mistake: designing the intervention around the wrong barrier.
IInsight Tools — Design the nudgeDesign▾
Select the behaviour change mechanism using MINDSPACE and EAST: Messenger, Incentive, Norms, Defaults, Salience, Priming, Affect, Commitment, or Ego. We design the intervention — messaging, choice architecture, environment redesign, digital nudge, or campaign — and state the testable hypothesis it rests on.
BBehavioural Experiment — Test before scaleValidation▾
Run a controlled pilot — field RCT, A/B test, or quasi-experimental design — comparing control (no intervention) against treatment (the nudge). Sample size is calculated for adequate statistical power. Ethics protocol is applied where required. We never recommend scaling an untested intervention.
RReflection — Scale what works. Iterate what doesn't.Scale▾
If the experiment confirms the hypothesis: we deliver a fully documented scale-up playbook — ready for your implementation team, leadership, or donors. If the hypothesis fails: we return to K with a revised hypothesis. KALIBER is a cycle, not a project. Most breakthroughs come on the second or third pass.

Behavioural Audit & Module Redesign · National Safety Standards
Indonesia's national road safety training tells drivers what to do. So why do fatal accidents keep happening?
The audit revealed drivers already know the rules. The real barriers are optimism bias ("accidents don't happen to me"), present bias (comfort over precaution), and social norms (non-compliance is normalised by peers). The redesign applied EAST and COM-B to all 5 modules: replacing 812 words of technical instruction in the brake failure module with a three-step visual heuristic — reducing cognitive load at the conditions that cause accidents.

4-phase structure

  • Phase 1 (Wk 1–3): Current State Assessment — gap analysis per user group across all 5 modules
  • Phase 2 (Wk 4–5): Behavioural Audit — score each module for clarity, salience, and nudge effectiveness using EAST + COM-B
  • Phase 3 (Wk 6–9): Validation — SKKNI alignment + survey comparing original vs redesigned for engagement and compliance motivation
  • Phase 4 (Wk 10–12): Presentation Materials — 5 redesigned decks with speaker notes

Key findings

  • Brake failure module: 812 words, 17 slides — extreme cognitive overload for truck and bus drivers
  • Redesign: 3-step visual heuristic ("3 Checks: Body, Brakes, Road") — tested against control condition
  • Dominant barrier: optimism bias and peer normalisation of non-compliance, not information deficit
  • Solution: commitment device (checklist in driver cabin) + narrative persona framing + social norm messaging

Indonesia's stunting rate is 21.5% — yet only 7.4% of caregivers attend the parenting classes that would fix it
Only 7.4% of eligible caregivers attended BKB parenting classes — and fathers were almost entirely absent. KALIBER audit revealed the barriers were not logistical: childcare is socially coded as women's work, and youth found BKB irrelevant and socially unappealing. 16 segment-specific nudge interventions were designed — "Save the Date" for female caregivers, social gamification for youth, identity-based visual messaging for fathers. Interventions are now embedded in national BKB cadre training under PERPRES No.72/2021, informing Indonesia's stunting acceleration strategy.

What happens after
you reach out.

Every engagement follows the same six-phase process. We tell you at the scoping call which phases apply — and how long each will take for your specific situation.

1

Scoping Call

30–45 minute conversation: your behaviour change challenge, target population, existing data, institutional constraints, timeline, and budget. We tell you which engagement format fits — and if we're not the right team, we say so.

⏱ Free · No commitment · 30–45 minutes
2

Proposal

Written proposal with KALIBER steps to be executed, methodology, phase-by-phase timeline, team composition, and two budget options (with and without experiment). Delivered within 5–7 working days.

⏱ 5–7 working days
3

Key Behaviour Definition

Stakeholder workshops to align everyone on the specific, measurable behaviour target. Prevents the most expensive mistake in behaviour change: collecting the wrong data about the wrong behaviour.

⏱ 1–2 weeks
4

Behavioural Audit & Factor Analysis

Field observation, FGD, interviews, segmentation, persona mapping, behaviour flow mapping, and leveraging/lowering factor ranking. This is where we find the real barrier — which is often not what the brief assumes.

⏱ 2–6 weeks depending on scope and geography

Intervention Design & Experiment

MINDSPACE/EAST tool selection, nudge design, and controlled pilot — A/B test, field experiment, or quasi-experimental design. Results report with statistical analysis. Never scale an untested intervention.

⏱ 3–10 weeks depending on method and engagement format
6

Scale-Up Playbook & Debrief

Fully documented implementation guide: intervention materials, monitoring framework, and rollout brief — ready for your team to execute independently. Leadership debrief and handoff session included.

⏱ Included in all engagements

Common
questions.

What's the difference between Research and Consultancy now that they're merged?+
The difference is how far through the KALIBER cycle you need us to go. The diagnostic steps (K→A→L→I) produce a behavioural evidence base — defining the behaviour, auditing it in context, identifying the leveraging factors, and selecting the appropriate insight tools. The validation and scale steps (B→R) use that evidence to test the intervention and produce a scale-up playbook. In a Rapid Audit, we stop after the diagnostic. In a Full Engagement or Research Partnership, we run the complete cycle. You don't have to choose between "research" and "consultancy" — describe your situation and we scope accordingly at the call.
How long does an engagement take from start to finish?+
Rapid Audit: 2–4 weeks. Full Engagement: up to 6 months. Research Partnership: minimum 12 months. These are ranges — we give you a phase-by-phase estimate at the scoping call based on your specific geography, population size, method requirements, and institutional approval timelines.
What's a typical budget range?+
Every engagement is individually scoped — cost depends on the number of behaviour targets, field geography, population size, experimental method, and output format required. We provide a budget estimate at the scoping call, alongside two options: one with a behavioural validation experiment and one without, so you can decide what level of evidence your situation requires. We will also tell you honestly if your budget is insufficient for the question you are asking — and suggest a scoped alternative if one exists.
Can the output be published academically or submitted to donors?+
Yes, for Research Partnership engagements using publishable-grade methodology. We produce a methodology write-up structured to journal submission standards. Our COVID-19 handwashing study was published in Analyses of Social Issues and Public Policy (2022) and received the IRSA-BKF Best Paper Award. Publication rights and authorship are agreed at the proposal stage.
Can you work outside Java?+
Yes. We have conducted engagements in NTT, Lombok (NTB), and Padang Lawas (Sumatera Utara). Our Indonesia Personality Map covers all 34 provinces. For outside-Java fieldwork, we use a vetted network of local field researchers trained on the KALIBER observation protocol and supervised centrally for methodological consistency.
We already have survey data. Can you build on it?+
Yes. Existing survey data often captures what is happening but not why. We use it as a background layer and add behavioural audit methodology to answer the causal question. We'll review your data at the scoping call and tell you what it can and cannot tell you — and what additional work would close the gap.
How is Nudgeplus different from a general management consultancy?+
Three ways. First, we diagnose before we design — we run a behavioural audit before proposing any intervention, because most interventions fail due to wrong diagnosis. Second, we use KALIBER — a proprietary 7-step protocol grounded in 60+ years of peer-reviewed behavioral science, not generic frameworks applied without calibration. Third, we test before we scale — every intervention we recommend has been piloted against a control condition. We never ask you to scale something we haven't verified moves the behaviour.
